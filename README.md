This is all the publicly available Ncodes I could find, extracted and normalized
for easy insertion into printable pages.

They're from two sources:
 * a4, a5, and letter codes are from https://neolab.net/en/customer/ncode-pdf
 * a4plus (which are labeled as A4 but are, in fact, bigger both than A4 and US
   Letter) are from https://store.neosmartpen.com/board/list.php?bdId=NcodePDF

Files are by paper size, then by Neo LAB's name for the template, e.g.
`letter/manuscript` is letter-sized manuscript paper, and `a4/letter` is
A4-sized letter-writing paper.

The `a4plus` pages are excellent for those of us in the US, because (a) they're
distinct codes from the US Letter equivalents, so you can use them at the same
time as their US Letter equivalents, and (b) they're bigger than both A4 and US
Letter, so you can use them on most normal paper sizes.
